# ClassApex LMS

## Register
product

## Users & Purpose
- **Primary**: Students and teachers in K-12 through university settings who need an intuitive, fast interface for course management, assignments, grades, and communication.
- **Context**: Users are task-oriented — checking grades, submitting work, reviewing course materials, monitoring activity. They use this daily as their primary learning interface.
- **Job**: Reduce friction between the user and their educational workflow. Canvas LMS backend is powerful but its UI is dated; ClassApex replaces the frontend while keeping Canvas as the data layer.

## Brand Personality
Precise, calm, capable

## Anti-References
- Traditional LMS UIs (cluttered, dated, information-dense without hierarchy)
- "SaaS-template" design (purple-to-blue gradients, Inter everywhere, rounded-square icons above every heading, gray text on colored backgrounds)

## Strategic Principles
1. **Content-forward**: The course material, grades, and assignments are the stars — chrome recedes.
2. **Consistent rhythm**: 8px grid, predictable spacing, every component tells you where the next one will be.
3. **Task-optimized**: Every screen answers one question — what needs attention right now?
4. **Accessible-first**: WCAG 2.1 AA minimum, designed for keyboard and screen reader users from the start.
